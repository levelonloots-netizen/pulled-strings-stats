# Pulled Strings Stats

Pulled Strings Stats is a private tool that reads the Pulled Strings Facebook Page and YouTube channel stats for its owner and saves them as text reports on the owner's own computer.

It is not a public service and is used only by the owner of the Pulled Strings page and channel.
