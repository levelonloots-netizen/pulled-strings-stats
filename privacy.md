# Pulled Strings Stats - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Pulled Strings Stats is a private tool used only by the owner of the Pulled Strings Facebook Page and YouTube channel.

## What it reads

It reads the view, watch-time, follower, subscriber and engagement stats of the owner's own Facebook Page and YouTube channel, plus the titles and dates of the owner's own posts and videos. It only asks for read-only access and cannot post, edit or delete anything.

## Where the data goes

Everything is saved as text files on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sent anywhere except to Facebook's and Google's own APIs to read the stats. No data is shared, sold or used for ads.

## Google user data

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

## Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions and in Facebook settings under Apps and Websites.
